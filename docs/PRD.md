# Product Requirements Document

## Executive Summary
Lifetime Fitness currently relies heavily on Instagram while its old website no longer serves as a trustworthy business asset. This demo showcases what a premium digital presence could look like.

## Problem Statement
Current pain points:
- Broken website experience
- Lost credibility
- No online lead funnel
- Poor SEO visibility
- No membership conversion system

## Business Goals
Increase:
- Walk-ins
- Trial bookings
- WhatsApp inquiries
- Brand trust
- Google rankings

## Target Audience
### Primary
- College students
- Young professionals
- Fitness beginners
- Athletes

### Secondary
- Personal training clients
- Women seeking safe gyms
- Weight-loss customers

## User Journey
Visitor lands -> Sees premium hero -> Views facilities -> Reads transformations -> Compares plans -> Clicks WhatsApp.
Goal: under 90 seconds.
