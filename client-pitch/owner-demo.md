# Sales Pitch & Owner Demo

## 30-Second Elevator Pitch
"Sir, I noticed your old website isn't representing the quality of your gym anymore. I built this premium concept to show how Lifetime could convert more visitors into memberships while strengthening trust on Google."

## Objection Handling
| Objection | Response |
| --- | --- |
| We already have Instagram | Instagram doesn't rank on Google like a website does. |
| Too expensive | One membership can recover a large part of the website cost. |
| We don't need it | Many customers search "gym near me" before visiting. |

## ROI Example
**Assumptions:**
- 300 website visitors/month
- 5% conversion
- 15 leads
- 4 memberships at ₹2,500/month each

**Revenue Generated:**
- Monthly: ₹10,000+
- Annual: ₹1.2 lakh+
